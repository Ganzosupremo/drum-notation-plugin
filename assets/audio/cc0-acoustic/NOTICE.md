# Bundled acoustic drum samples

These selected one-shots are derived from the `ferrosintesis-samples-drumkit`
0.2.0 and `ferrosintesis-samples-drumkit2` 0.1.0 crates.

- Core kit source: Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios / Karoryfer Samples.
- Crash and splash source: Virtuosity Drums.
- China source: Big Rusty Drums by Karoryfer Samples.
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal. The complete legal text is in `LICENSE-CC0`.
- Upstream preparation and provenance:
  <https://github.com/0x4D44/ferrosintesis>

The plugin embeds a small curated subset so BRAT installations remain
self-contained and work offline.
